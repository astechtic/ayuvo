# Scientific methods and references

Every analytics algorithm in `shared/analytics` and the derived metrics it relies on, with the source that supports it, what that source actually validates, its limitations and what Ayuvo uses it for. References were checked on 2026-10-07 against Crossref and PubMed (DOIs, PMIDs) and the vendors' developer documentation; an entry that could not be confirmed would be marked as Ayuvo design instead. The machine-readable list is `references` in `shared/analytics/analytics_config.json` (each with `verified`, `supports`, `limitations`) and is rendered in docs/health-analytics.md §11.

Wording rule: no algorithm here is "scientifically authorized" or clinically validated *for Ayuvo's use*. A reference supports the method; it does not validate Ayuvo's implementation on consumer-device data (see docs/health-validation.md).

## Ayuvo design choices (no external source)

- Recovery v2 weights (HRV 35, resting HR 25, sleep 25, respiratory rate 5, temperature 5, blood oxygen 5), the subscore mapping 50 + 20·z and the bands 67/34. Each weight has a stated reason in config `recovery.components[].why`.
- Spread floors per metric, baseline windows and minimum points, the confidence formula and caps per classification.
- Sleep need clamp 7–9 h and the 14-night minimum; sleep-debt window of 14 recorded nights.
- Load-state percentiles (95/90/80/10) and guards (ratio ≥ 1.3 / 1.2, acute vs median 1.1 / 0.8).
- Multi-signal thresholds (|z| ≥ 2 mild, ≥ 3 significant, ≥ 3 signals multi) and the 3-day persistence rule.
- Correlation surfacing (q ≤ 0.1 and |ρ| ≥ 0.3, at least 21 pairs) and the forecast gate (≥ 5% better than both baselines).
- Source policy: wearable device first, then most samples, then source id; 60 s / ±0.5 or 1% Google Health de-duplication window.

## Corrections found while verifying

- TRIMP for women used 0.64·e^(1.67x); Banister (1991) gives 0.86·e^(1.67x). Fixed in derived contract v3 and workout contract v2.
- Shetler 2001 DOI is 10.1016/S0735-1097(01)01652-7.
- Reed & Sacco 2016 is cited for the TST/TIB convention it discusses, not as an endorsement of it.
- Watson 2015 recommends ≥ 7 h; the 9 h cap is Ayuvo's.
- Compendium MET bands are cited to WHO 2020; the Compendium paper supplies the per-activity values.
