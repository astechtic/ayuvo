# Code of conduct

Ayuvo is a small project about people's health, so we keep the space respectful and practical.

- Be kind and assume good faith. Disagree about the work, not the person.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or unwelcome contact.
- Do not share other people's health information, keys or private data, in issues, pull requests or screenshots.
- Keep health discussion about how the software behaves. Do not give medical advice to other users.

Maintainers may edit, close or remove contributions that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders.
Report a concern privately to <yaaratech@gmail.com>.
