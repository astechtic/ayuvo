01 Features

Everything in one place.

Six parts of your health that usually live in six apps. Ayuvo keeps them together, offline first, and lets an AI coach read across all of them.

01 · Nutrition

Log meals your way.

Photo · Barcode · Voice · Text · Saved meals · Manual

Photos (up to 10 at once), barcode via Open Food Facts, voice, text, saved meals or manual entry. Review calories, macros and 30+ nutrients before anything is saved, and ask "What if?" before you log.

  • Smart serving units — slices, cups, ml — with grams as the source of truth
  • Personalised calorie and macro targets (Mifflin-St Jeor / Katch-McArdle) you can override
  • Custom meal times, nutrient goals, Home nutrient cards
02 · Workouts

Plan the week, log the set.

Diary · Sets, reps, weight, RPE · 1,300+ exercises

A day-by-day workout diary with sets, reps, weight and RPE, calculated calorie burn, and a 1,300-exercise library with photos and animations, filtered by body part, target muscle and equipment.

  • Swipe between weeks, copy plans, log without a timer
  • Calculated burn can sync to Apple Health / Health Connect
  • The coach can read your plans, sets and completed sessions
03 · Health data

Your Health data, one hub.

Apple Health · Health Connect · Local mirror

Mirror Apple Health or Health Connect — activity, heart, sleep, vitals, body, cycle and more — into a local hub with Home tiles, detail charts and full history. Read-only unless you log something in Ayuvo.

  • Day, week, month, six-month and year charts with highlights
  • Show All Data, Data Sources and unit options per metric
  • Export and import the whole hub as a zip; it never rides in cloud backups
04 · Fasting & water

Timers that respect your day.

1–168 h fasting goals · Water goals · Reminders

Optional fasting with a 1–168 hour goal, a timer that survives restarts and editable history; optional water goals with quick logging, reminders and widgets. Both stay local and are never written to Health.

  • Off by default — turn each on in Settings when you want it
  • Fasting is never inferred from missing meals
  • Water progress on Apple Watch and in widgets
05 · AI Coach

A coach that knows your day.

Multi-turn chat · Tool calls over your own data

Multi-turn chat that can pull your food log, workouts, weight trend, fasts and — only with your explicit toggle — your health data. Choose from 15 providers, or run Gemma 4 / Ollama locally.

  • Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, OpenRouter, Together AI, Groq, Hugging Face, Fireworks AI, DeepInfra, Mistral, DeepSeek, Cerebras, Ollama or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint
  • Keys live in iOS Keychain / Android EncryptedSharedPreferences; requests go straight to the provider
  • Apple Intelligence and on-device Gemma 4 E2B send nothing anywhere
06 · Privacy

Private by construction.

No account · No server · No analytics

There is no Ayuvo account or server. Requests go straight from your phone to the provider you chose. Optional iCloud / Google Drive backup never includes health data. Delete All Data wipes the device copy in one tap.

What can leave your phone, and only when you act

  • Photos, text and coach messages → the AI provider you configured
  • Audio → the speech provider you chose (or nothing, on-device)
  • A barcode number → Open Food Facts, when you scan
  • Exercise images and model downloads → GitHub and Hugging Face, as plain file requests
  • Your backup → your own iCloud or Google Drive, if you turn it on
02 How it works

From capture to coaching in seconds.

Step 01

Capture

Snap up to 10 photos, scan a barcode, speak, type, or reuse a saved meal. Log a set, a glass of water, or start a fast from the same + menu.

Step 02

Review

Your provider identifies the food and estimates the nutrition. Correct anything, adjust servings, preview the impact on today's targets.

Step 03

Log

Saved on your device. Nutrition, body metrics and calculated burn sync to Apple Health / Health Connect only if you enable it.

Step 04

Ask Ayuvo

The coach pulls the slice of your data it needs — food, training, weight trend, sleep or heart with your toggle — and answers in context.

04 FAQ

Ayuvo, plainly answered.

Is Ayuvo free?

Yes. There are no subscriptions, credits or in-app purchases. AI features use an API key you create with a provider (many have free tiers); any usage is billed by that provider, never by Ayuvo.

Which AI can I use?

Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, OpenRouter, Together AI, Groq, Hugging Face, Fireworks AI, DeepInfra, Mistral, DeepSeek, Cerebras, Ollama or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. On supported phones Gemma 4 E2B runs fully on-device after a one-time download; iPhones can also use Apple Intelligence as a fallback.

Where is my data?

In the app's storage on your phone. Ayuvo has no account and no server. Data leaves the device only when you act: sending a photo or message to your AI provider, scanning a barcode, browsing exercise images, downloading a model, or turning on backup.

What does the Health data hub do with Apple Health or Health Connect?

With your permission it keeps a local mirror of the data types you grant so Ayuvo can show tiles, charts and history. It writes only nutrition, body measurements and calculated workout calories that you log. You can revoke access any time in Health or Health Connect settings.

Does the coach see my health data?

Only if you turn on "Let Coach use my health data". When on, the coach can request summaries of your granted health types and sends them to the AI provider you chose. The toggle is shown at every connect step and in Settings.

How do backups work?

Off by default. iPhone backs up to your private iCloud (CloudKit) database; Android to your Google Drive app folder. Backups include logs, settings and photos but never the health hub database or coach chat.

Can I export or delete everything?

Export your diary (JSON, CSV or Markdown) and your health hub (ayuvo-health-data zip). Settings → Delete All Data wipes the app's local storage; Health and Health Connect samples are left untouched by design.

Which languages does Ayuvo support?

18: English, Arabic, Azerbaijani, Czech, Dutch, French, German, Hindi, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil), Romanian, Russian, Simplified Chinese, Spanish and Ukrainian.

Is it medical advice?

No. Estimates come from AI and formulas; talk to a clinician before changing diet, training or medication.

How do I get support?

Email yaaratech@gmail.com or open the Support page. In the app, Settings → Help & Support opens a pre-filled email.
